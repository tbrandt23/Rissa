Visual Language

A working reference for the personal site. Dark, warm, editorial — with three deliberate moves that pull the design out of the AI-default zone: an off-center composition, a single italic serif accent, and a fine film grain across every surface.

05

Principles

Do
  • Let whitespace carry the page
  • Use weight, not color, for hierarchy
  • Hairline borders, never thick
  • Hover = opacity transition only
  • Trust the typography
  • Vary section rhythm on Creative page
Don't
  • Add chromatic accents or gradients
  • Use drop shadows or filled buttons
  • Round corners beyond 2px
  • Use emoji or stock icons as decoration
  • Show platform branding anywhere
  • Treat every section the same
06

Distinctive Moves

Three deliberate decisions that pull the site out of the AI-default zone. Skipping any one of them collapses the personality.

Move 01

Off-center homepage

Wordmark anchored lower-left, metadata floats upper-right. Composition reads as magazine cover, not landing page. The asymmetry is the point — centered hero is the AI-default tell.

Move 02

One italic accent

Exactly one word in Fraunces italic, used only in the homepage descriptor: curation. Its rarity is what makes it work. Do not repeat this move anywhere else on the site.

Move 03

Film grain overlay

A fine SVG noise texture at 3.5% opacity across every surface of the site. You're looking at it now — visible on this page. Makes the dark backgrounds feel printed rather than rendered. The single biggest move against the surgical-flat AI aesthetic.
